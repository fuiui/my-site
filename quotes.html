LEARNING & REFLECTION

A few words. A lasting thought.

Chinese originals are shown in simplified characters. The English lines are plain-language renderings prepared for this site with AI assistance, not quotations from a published translation.

01

Begin

千里之行，始于足下。

A journey of a thousand miles begins beneath your feet.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 64 ↗
02

Practise

学而时习之，不亦说乎？

Isn't it a pleasure to learn and regularly practise what you have learned?

ConfuciusThe Analects, 1.1 ↗
03

Revisit

温故而知新，可以为师矣。

Someone who finds new understanding by revisiting what they have learned can be a teacher.

ConfuciusThe Analects, 2.11 ↗
04

Reflect

学而不思则罔，思而不学则殆。

Learning without reflection leaves us confused; reflection without learning leaves us on uncertain ground.

ConfuciusThe Analects, 2.15 ↗
05

Look inward

知人者智，自知者明。

Understanding others takes wisdom; understanding yourself brings clarity.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 33 ↗

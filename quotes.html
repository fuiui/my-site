LEARNING & REFLECTION

A few words. A lasting thought.

Chinese originals are shown in simplified characters. The English lines are plain-language renderings prepared for this site with AI assistance, not quotations from a published translation.

01

Begin

千里之行，始于足下。

A journey of a thousand miles begins beneath your feet.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 64 ↗
02

Practise

学而时习之，不亦说乎？

Isn't it a pleasure to learn and regularly practise what you have learned?

ConfuciusThe Analects, 1.1 ↗
03

Revisit

温故而知新，可以为师矣。

Someone who finds new understanding by revisiting what they have learned can be a teacher.

ConfuciusThe Analects, 2.11 ↗
04

Reflect

学而不思则罔，思而不学则殆。

Learning without reflection leaves us confused; reflection without learning leaves us on uncertain ground.

ConfuciusThe Analects, 2.15 ↗
05

Look inward

知人者智，自知者明。

Understanding others takes wisdom; understanding yourself brings clarity.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 33 ↗
06

Like water

上善若水。水善利万物而不争。

The highest goodness is like water: it benefits all things without competing.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 8 ↗
07

Inner strength

胜人者有力，自胜者强。

Overcoming others takes strength; overcoming yourself is true strength.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 33 ↗
08

Enough

知足者富。

Those who know what is enough are rich.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 33 ↗
09

Quiet depth

大音希声，大象无形。

The greatest sound is scarcely heard; the greatest image has no fixed form.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 41 ↗
10

Start small

图难于其易，为大于其细。

Approach difficult things while they are easy, and great things while they are small.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 63 ↗
11

The details

天下难事，必作于易，天下大事，必作于细。

Difficult tasks begin with easy steps; great undertakings begin with small details.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 63 ↗
12

Keep your word

夫轻诺必寡信，多易必多难。

Promises made lightly seldom earn trust; treating everything as easy brings difficulties.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 63 ↗
13

Grow

合抱之木，生于毫末；九层之台，起于累土。

A tree too broad to embrace grows from a tiny shoot; a nine-storey terrace rises from piled earth.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 64 ↗
14

Finish with care

慎终如始，则无败事。

Be as careful at the end as at the beginning, and avoid spoiling the work.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 64 ↗
15

Learn and simplify

为学日益，为道日损。

In learning, add something each day; in following the Dao, let something go each day.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 48 ↗
16

Listen

知者不言，言者不知。

Those who know do not speak; those who speak do not know.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 56 ↗
17

Speak truly

信言不美，美言不信。

Truthful words are not always beautiful; beautiful words are not always truthful.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 81 ↗
18

Know when to stop

知足不辱，知止不殆，可以长久。

Knowing enough avoids disgrace; knowing when to stop avoids danger and allows things to endure.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 44 ↗
19

Beyond appearances

大直若屈，大巧若拙，大辩若讷。

Great straightness can look bent, great skill can look clumsy, and great eloquence can sound hesitant.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 45 ↗
20

Leave no trace

善行无辙迹，善言无瑕谪。

Skilful travel leaves no tracks; skilful speech leaves no fault to find.

Laozi (traditional attribution)Dao De Jing, chapter 27 ↗

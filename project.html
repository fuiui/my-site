PROJECT NOTEBOOK · FIVE POSSIBILITIES

Small ideas.
A place to start.

A useful website starts with someone to build it for. These are five possible directions to compare before choosing a personal project.

Explore the ideas ↓

01 / EXPLORE

Things I might build.

Working ideas prepared with AI assistance. The subject, content and final choice still need personal review.

  • Study Corner

    A compact revision guide with topic summaries, a glossary and a short self-check quiz.

  • Campus First Steps

    A guide to getting settled on campus, with practical checklists and useful places.

  • Small Kitchen

    A collection of simple meals for one person, with a serving-size calculator.

  • Everyday Frames

    A photography journal organised by subject, with a filterable gallery.

  • Game Starter Notes

    A beginner's guide to one game, with basic controls, vocabulary and a quick knowledge check.

02 / FIND A READER

Who each one is for.

Study Corner
Classmates who need a quick way to revisit introductory web concepts before a lesson or exam.
Campus First Steps
New students looking for a manageable checklist during their first week on campus.
Small Kitchen
Students cooking for one who want short ingredient lists and adjustable portions.
Everyday Frames
Friends and fellow beginner photographers interested in small observations from everyday life.
Game Starter Notes
First-time players of a chosen game who want to understand its basics without story spoilers.

03 / A POSSIBLE START

Study Corner: a working direction.

This is a proposal to evaluate, not a final choice. It could start small, with four pages and no accounts or database.

  • Home — explain who the guide helps and where to start.
  • Topics — short explanations of HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Glossary — essential terms with small examples.
  • Practice — a short quiz that gives immediate feedback.

Build first: the Topics page, so the central explanations are useful before adding more features.

Smallest useful version: four linked pages, three topic summaries and a five-question quiz that works on a phone.

04 / MAKE IT REAL

How I will build it.

  1. Choose a subject and confirm who the website is for.
  2. Collect original or properly sourced content and sketch the four pages.
  3. Write the HTML, starting with the most useful page.
  4. Add consistent CSS and check the layout on a phone.
  5. Add one interaction, test the links and publish the finished version.

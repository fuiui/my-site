01 / WHAT I AM LEARNING

Understand the pieces.

My goal is to understand how a page works, not only how it looks. The exercises on this site bring three parts of web development together.

HTML: give content meaning
Use headings, paragraphs, lists and quotations to describe what each piece of content is.
CSS: make content readable
Work with colour, type and spacing, and make the same content fit a phone or a larger screen.
JavaScript: connect the pieces
Start with loading shared navigation and a footer, then explore interactive features in later exercises.

02 / WHAT IS HERE

A small site, built in stages.

  • Home introduces the website and links to class resources.
  • Quotes collects twenty classical Chinese quotations with English renderings, sources and a random-quote feature.
  • Ideas compares five possible personal projects and the people they could help.
  • This About page explains the learning goals and how the website is being developed.

The site is still in progress. The quote collection now includes a random display; further interactive exercises are planned for later stages.

03 / HOW IT IS MADE

Build, read, check, improve.

This website uses HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript. Its shared navigation and footer live in separate files, so a change can appear on every page.

Codex has assisted with code, design and English wording. My learning goal is to read the code, understand the decisions and make my own changes. AI assistance does not replace understanding the work.

The quote page names its original sources and labels the English lines as renderings prepared for this site. The illustration and ZM icons were created for this project with AI assistance.
